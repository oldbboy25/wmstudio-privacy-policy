WM Studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026
In short: WM Studio processes every photo and video entirely on your device. We do not collect, upload, store, or share any of your photos, videos, or personal information — because the app never sends them anywhere.

What this app is

WM Studio is an Android app for removing watermarks and logos from photos and videos, and for editing video (trimming, adding music, text, filters, and effects). This policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data we collect

None. WM Studio does not have a server, does not require an account or sign-in, and does not use any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting service. The app contains no code that transmits your photos, videos, audio, or any other data over the internet. All editing — blurring, pixelating, cropping, trimming, adding text or music, and exporting — happens using your device's own processor and storage.

Permissions the app requests, and why

  • Photos and videos (media access) — so you can pick a photo or video from your device to edit. The app only reads the specific file you choose.
  • Microphone — only used if you choose to record audio directly inside the video editor (an optional feature). The recording is saved to your device like any other clip; it is never transmitted anywhere.
  • Storage (save/export) — used to save your edited photo or video back to your device's gallery.

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit data off your device. You can review or revoke any of these permissions at any time in your device's Settings.

Where your files go

Every photo or video you open stays on your device for the entire editing session. Exported files are written to your device's own gallery/storage. WM Studio has no cloud storage, no upload step, and no remote server of any kind — there is simply nowhere else for your files to go.

Third-party services

WM Studio does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The app is built only on standard Android system libraries and on-device media processing components.

Children's privacy

WM Studio does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, since it does not collect information at all. The app is not directed at children and contains no functionality that requires providing personal information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds an optional feature that requires network access — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
